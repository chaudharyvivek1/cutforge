# v2.8 candidates — open findings against v2.7

All six findings from the previous list are resolved in v2.7. What follows is what v2.7 leaves open, and the list has changed character again: the remaining gaps are no longer about the service surface, they are about the engine having no assets.

## Summary

| # | Finding | Touches | Severity | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | There is no asset table and no ingest pipeline | §10, §13.3 | High | Missing subsystem |
| 2 | Storage quota is unenforceable without assets | §16.1 | High | Consequence of §1 |
| 3 | Analysis is entirely unimplemented | §10 | High | Missing subsystem |
| 4 | Three GPU signals remain unemitted | §17 | Medium | Blocked on hardware |
| 5 | Webhook ordering is not guaranteed and not stated | §15.3 | Low | Defect |

## 1 — There is no asset table and no ingest pipeline

**Severity: high, and it is now the single largest gap.**

The compiler assumes conformed assets exist. Nothing creates one: no upload, no probe, no proxy, no conform, no derivative bookkeeping, no `assets` row. §10 and §13.3 specify all of it and none of it has run.

Every other gap in this repository is downstream of this one. The renderer cannot be tested at 4K without media, the planner cannot be evaluated without a corpus, and storage cannot be quoted without a table.

**Proposed resolution.** The asset table, a probe on upload, a proxy and a conform per asset, and a test that a 25 fps upload on a 29.97 timeline produces the conformed derivative §5.9 assumes. That is ordinary work with no research in it.

## 2 — Storage quota is unenforceable without assets

**Severity: high, and it is a consequence of finding 1 rather than an independent defect.**

`consume_render_minutes()` enforces the quota that costs GPU time. The other half of §16.1 — stored bytes — needs an asset table to sum. It is deferred and named, which is the right treatment, and it stays open until assets exist.

## 3 — Analysis is entirely unimplemented

**Severity: high.**

§10 specifies shot detection, transcription, speaker diarisation, object tags, face embeddings and a contact sheet. None exists. The planner takes shots as given, which means the prompt→plan path has never seen a real transcript.

**Proposed resolution.** Start with shot detection and transcription on a synthetic fixture — the two the planner actually consumes. Embeddings can wait for v3.1, because the feature they serve is gated anyway.

## 4 — Three GPU signals remain unemitted

**Severity: medium, and honestly blocked.**

GPU memory, encoder utilisation and success rate by kind need a GPU worker. Emitting them as constants would be worse than their absence, so they stay specified and unemitted.

## 5 — Webhook ordering is not guaranteed and not stated

**Severity: low.**

Deliveries drain in creation order within a run, and a retried delivery can overtake a later one. §15.3 says receivers must be idempotent on event id and does not say that ordering is not promised — which is the kind of omission that becomes an argument with a customer.

**Proposed resolution.** One sentence in §15.3: ordering is not guaranteed; a receiver must not depend on it. Cheap, and it converts a latent disagreement into a documented constraint.

## 6 — The usage period has no rollover test

**Severity: low.**

`consume_render_minutes()` keys on `%Y-%m`, and nothing tests that a new month starts from zero. A quota that never resets is a quota that eventually refuses everything.

**Proposed resolution.** Pass a period explicitly in a test and assert that consumption in one period does not affect another.

## How this list is maintained

A finding enters when it is noticed and substantiated — a section number, an arithmetic error, or two rules that cannot both hold. It leaves when a version resolves it, and the resolution is named in that version's §0 table. It is never quietly dropped.

The character has changed four times now: contradictions (v1.1–v1.5), defects in new code (v1.6–v1.7), defects in the artefact (v1.8), the distance to a running engine (v1.9–v2.7), and now **a missing subsystem**. That progression is the clearest statement of where the project actually is, and `ROADMAP.md` turns it into an order of work.
